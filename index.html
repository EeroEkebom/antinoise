How it's constructed

Traffic Sensor towerhears the rumble first Optical fiberanalysis is faster than sound — the whole idea ControllerFXLMS filter + emission scheduler Cancel arrayanti-noise timed to meet the wavefront Quiet zoneextends as far as the wavelength allows

The controller must emit the anti-noise (traveling darray→zone) so it meets the ambient wavefront (traveling droad→zone) exactly — the head start between tower reference and zone arrival is the lead the fiber buys: lead = (dzone − dtower − dstandoff) / 343 m/s. Deep rumble (long wavelength) cancels across a region; short waves only in a narrow spot — that's why this field targets low frequencies by physics, while headphones (your eardrum) can go much higher.
